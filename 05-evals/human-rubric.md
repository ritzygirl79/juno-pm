## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s weekly ATO User Story Proposal
- **Value proposition:** Each week, Juno turns customer, prospect, and team signals into the 5 highest-impact user stories for the ATO PM to review Monday morning in JIRA dashboard. They are ranked by potential revenue and feature impact, and each links to a source I can verify.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Do all 5 user stories include the source, and each source clearly supports the story. | 1 to 5 | Any fabricated source, materially unsupported factual claim or invented customer information. | All material claims are supported, but some noncritical evidence is incomplete and explicitly flagged. | Every material factual claim has a valid source, record ID and timestamp. Observations and inferences are clearly distinguished |
| Signal grounding & recency | Do the stories come from this week’s signals across customer, prospect, and team inputs? | 1 to 5 | 1 — Poor	Most proposed stories lack dated supporting evidence, rely primarily on outdated information, double-count customer signals, or present historical memory as current activity. | All proposed stories include source counts, signal types and dates. Most are grounded in the seven-day reporting window, but some have incomplete historical context, insufficient cross-source corroboration or minor deduplication issues. Limitations are disclosed. | All proposed stories are grounded in relevant signals from the seven days preceding the Monday run. Historical evidence is clearly labeled and used to establish recurring themes or trends. Reference data is current and verified. Duplicate signals are consolidated, and inferred findings are supported by at least two independent data points. |
| Prioritization Rational | Is the revenue and feature impact reasoning explicit, reasonable, and consistent with the ranking?  | 1 to 5 | 3 or more stories are missing revenue or renewal impact, or there is no score or rationale, or the ranking looks arbitrary. | All 5 stories include revenue and renewal impact, but 1 or 2 are missing the quarterly objective or issue type priority, or one pair of stories is out of score order with no explanation. | All 5 stories state revenue impact (including renewal exposure for the current and upcoming quarter, or explicitly “none”), name the quarterly objective and issue type priority, and show the score inputs. Rank, revenue impact, issue type priority, source count, and source link are all visible in the list view, and the full review takes 30 minutes or less. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** we will create a baseline for the first 4 weeks and score every weekly report and going forward we would do it every other week.
- **Who scores them with you:** ATO PM and PM from the identity security team
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** Any disagreement on a critical-fail (fabricated or unsupporting source): always discuss. Open the source together and settle it on evidence, not opinion or if Scores differ by 2 or more points on any dimension: discuss and resolve.

## Pass bar

No critical-fail.  Dimension 4 and 5 is Pass. No dimension below 3, and the average of Dimensions 1, 2, 3, and 6 is at least 3.5.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any score of 1
- Usefulness: any "no"
- Security, Privacy and Governance.: any "no"
