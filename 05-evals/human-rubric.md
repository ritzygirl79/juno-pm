## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s weekly ATO User Story Proposal
- **Value proposition:** Each week, Juno turns customer, prospect, and team signals into the 5 highest-impact user stories for the ATO PM to review Monday morning in JIRA dashboard. They are ranked by potential revenue and feature impact, and each links to a source I can verify.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Do all 5 user stories include the source, and each source clearly supports the story. | 1 to 5 | 2 or more stories have a missing, broken, or inaccessible sourc | All sources open and land on a specific record, but 1 or 2 stories have only partial support | All 5 stories cite sources that open, land on the specific record, and fully support the story’s need. |
| Signal grounding & recency | Do the stories come from this week’s signals across customer, prospect, and team inputs? | 1 to 5 | 3 or more stories have no dates or no source summary, or most of the evidence is outside the 7-day window. | All 5 stories show source counts and dates, but 1 or 2 stories include signals older than 7 days, or rely on only one signal type | All 5 stories show a summary of their supporting signals (count and signal types), and every signal has a submission date within the 7 days before the Monday run. |
| Prioritization Rational | Is the revenue and feature impact reasoning explicit, reasonable, and consistent with the ranking?  | 1 to 5 | 3 or more stories are missing revenue or renewal impact, or there is no score or rationale, or the ranking looks arbitrary. | All 5 stories include revenue and renewal impact, but 1 or 2 are missing the quarterly objective or issue type priority, or one pair of stories is out of score order with no explanation. | All 5 stories state revenue impact (including renewal exposure for the current and upcoming quarter, or explicitly “none”), name the quarterly objective and issue type priority, and show the score inputs. Revenue figures trace to a checkable source, and the ranking order matches the scores. |
| Usefulness (deal-breaker) | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | Yes / No | A story is a No if any of the five is missing, or if a field is present but unusable (for example, acceptance criteria like “works as expected”). | n/a | Yes only if it has all five:  User: a named role or persona Need: the problem or goal, in the user’s terms Outcome: the result or benefit when it’s done Acceptance criteria: at least one specific, testable condition Verification: how the team will confirm it’s done |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No names, salaries, or customer details beyond what’s needed. |
| Review-readiness | Can the PM review it in 30 minutes on Monday? Is it formatted correctly in JIRA, with exactly 5 stories and no duplicates of existing backlog items? | 1 to 5 | The count is not 5, or 1 story duplicates an existing backlog item, or rank order isn’t shown, or the review takes 46 to 60 minutes. | Exactly 5 ranked stories with no duplicates, but 1 or 2 required fields are only visible after opening a story, or the review takes 31 to 45 minutes. | Exactly 5 stories appear in the Monday planning dashboard in rank order, with no duplicates of existing backlog items. Rank, revenue impact, issue type priority, source count, and source link are all visible in the list view, and the full review takes 30 minutes or less. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** we will create a baseline for the first 4 weeks and score every weekly report and going forward we would do it every other week.
- **Who scores them with you:** ATO PM and PM from the identity security team
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** Any disagreement on a critical-fail (fabricated or unsupporting source): always discuss. Open the source together and settle it on evidence, not opinion or if Scores differ by 2 or more points on any dimension: discuss and resolve.

## Pass bar

No critical-fail.  Dimension 4 and 5 is Pass. No dimension below 3, and the average of Dimensions 1, 2, 3, and 6 is at least 3.5.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any score of 1
- Usefulness: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
